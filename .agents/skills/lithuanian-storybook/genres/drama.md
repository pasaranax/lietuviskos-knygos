# Drama and everyday life — optional character module

Build a central tension from incompatible needs, loyalties, habits, or expectations. Give each important character a comprehensible perspective. Change relationships through actions and consequences, not explanatory speeches or artificial misunderstandings that one obvious sentence would resolve.

Develop changes in understanding, trust, commitment or practical circumstances across the story; quieter scenes can establish experience and rhythm. Stakes may be small externally and important personally. Quiet or episodic stories do not require a crime, emergency, villain, or cliffhanger.

Use recurring places and routines to revisit A2 vocabulary while changing the emotional context. Show emotions with concrete behavior, dialogue, and small decisions.
