# Drama and everyday life — optional character module

Build a central tension from incompatible needs, loyalties, habits, or expectations. Give each important character a comprehensible perspective. Change relationships through actions and consequences, not explanatory speeches or artificial misunderstandings that one obvious sentence would resolve.

Every scene should alter understanding, trust, commitment, or a practical situation. Stakes may be small externally and important personally. Quiet or episodic stories do not require a crime, emergency, villain, or cliffhanger.

Use recurring places and routines to revisit A2 vocabulary while changing the emotional context. Show emotions with concrete behavior, dialogue, and small decisions. A pilot offers a recognizable person, a specific tension, and an interaction whose consequence invites further reading.
