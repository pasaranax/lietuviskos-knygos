# Romance — optional relationship module

Give both people goals, agency, and reasons for attraction beyond appearance. Develop intimacy through shared experiences, attention, vulnerability, and choices. Obstacles should grow from credible needs or circumstances; repeated avoidable miscommunication cannot carry the entire arc.

Track changes in trust and what each person knows or assumes. Respect the requested tone and boundaries. Do not silently make a relationship subplot the whole story. If genre expectations about a hopeful ending matter, state the chosen default in the brief.

A2 dialogue can express interest, invitations, hesitation, disagreement, and care using ordinary words. Let repeated phrases acquire new meaning through context. A pilot establishes chemistry or relational tension and one small change; no external mystery is required.
