# Comedy — optional tone or central-conflict module

Ground humor in character goals, incompatible expectations, timing, escalation, and callbacks. Establish the normal pattern before breaking it. Let comic consequences change the situation; avoid a sequence of unrelated jokes or humiliating a character without purpose.

At A2, prefer visible situations and understandable reversals over obscure idioms, culturally unexplained references, or wordplay requiring advanced Lithuanian. Vocabulary repetition can support a callback, but the story must remain comprehensible without a Russian explanation of why it is funny.

When combined with other genres, decide the tonal boundary: humor can soften a scene without invalidating danger, grief, or intimacy. A pilot should demonstrate the particular comic relationship or predicament and deliver at least one earned payoff.
