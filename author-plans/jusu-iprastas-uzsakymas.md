# Jūsų įprastas užsakymas — story plan
2026-09-05. Book jusu-iprastas-uzsakymas. Title Jūsų įprastas užsakymas. Original AI-authored adult A2 pilot, Lithuanian narrative/Russian contextual notes. 12 chapters planned, ~800 words each (~9600 total). Modules: near-future setting; drama central conflict (comfort vs making one's own choices after breakup); comedy tonal support (routine automation vs an untidy life).

## Causal arc and ending
Tomas has delegated practical routines because he wants a fresh start, but has a habit of accepting offered defaults to avoid uncomfortable choices. A paid apartment assistant can order food, remind appointments and send messages from linked phone only under accepted household settings; it cannot read minds, unlock doors, spend without enabled budget or invent personal knowledge. An inherited weekly shared-dinner setting, accepted during hurried onboarding, paired a new resident with another consenting local participant, Ieva. His phone consent is real but inattentive; this is an explanation resolved early, not a conspiracy or mystery engine. Ieva seeks predictable company while caring for her father nearby, not a replacement partner. Her own time matters. The shared routines are useful but cannot decide what people owe one another. Endpoint: paid month expires; Tomas retains useful reminders but disables automatic personal invitations, cooks a modest meal himself, asks Ieva explicitly on a mutually convenient day, accepts that she cannot always come. She joins as an intentional equal friend with room for future intimacy, no obligatory romance or reunion. There is a cost: he learns to cook, plan transport and ask directly, accepts imperfect evenings alone. No all-powerful hidden assistant, no villains.

## Chapter skeleton (~800 words each)
1. New flat: goal settle and eat; obstacle boxes, empty fridge, rushed setup; choice accept assistant/use dinner order; two portions plus Ieva arrive; he chooses to listen/open door, first small act of agency. Establish food ordering, messages authorized with phone connection, month paid. Lexicon butas, raktas, kaimynas, užsakymas, porcija, čekis, kvietimas; need/request and accusative/genitive government.
2. Dinner: goal clarify without insulting guest; obstacle both tired, different assumptions; choice compare settings with her consent; explanation accepted-default shared meal is found; decide finish dinner, cancel automatic repeat, no crime investigation. Recur order/receipt/invitation, food requests.
3. Morning: goal punctual work; obstacle missed familiar route; choose ask neighbor instead of guessing; arrive slightly late but take responsibility. Transport/time, reminders; introduce coworker Rasa and clear working hours. Reminder cannot change buses.
4. Shopping: goal cook affordable meal; obstacle old two-person shopping habits; choose simple one-person menu, ask shop worker for quantity; one edible dinner. Food/amounts/prices, portions repeated.
5. Neighbor: goal return borrowed pan; obstacle neighbor needs small practical help at inconvenient hour; choose bounded commitment after work, keep it. Neighbor/routine and agreements; no endless self-sacrifice.
6. Saturday: goal collect remaining things from ex (Austėja); obstacle temptation to resume old roles; choose a concrete pickup window and return her key, respectful sadness, no villain. Keys/home/time, limits.
7. Invitation: goal see Ieva again; obstacle she must help father; direct invitation gets a no for tonight; choose ask another time without pressure, eat alone. Invitation/future/time, no manufactured misunderstanding.
8. Work: goal finish task before promised errand; obstacle extra request from colleague; specify realistic time; take bus in time; trust via kept small promise. Work/arrangements, polite refusal.
9. Shared kitchen: goal cook together on agreed day; obstacle different food preferences and forgotten ingredient; choose ask and improvise simple available food, learn preferences through conversation. Food/portion/orders and agency, earned comic payoff.
10. Bill: goal decide renewal before paid month ends; obstacle convenience vs cost; inspect actual services and choose reminders only, make manual shopping list; bounded tech choice not wholesale rejection. Receipt/payment/month and quantities.
11. Practice: goal host neighbor and Ieva; obstacle schedules don't align; choose smaller separate plans, cook test meal solo; accept limits, no deus ex machina. Invitations/agreements repeated.
12. Own order: goal first evening beyond paid month; obstacle no automatic dinner; follows list, cooks, personally invites at agreed time, friend arrives; ordinary meal with self-chosen routine, ending fulfills first two-portion opening. No perfection or reward romance.
